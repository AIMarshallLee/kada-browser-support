KADA Web to Data Privacy Policy

Kunlun Growth · Version 0.1.0

When you click Find tables, the extension looks for visible HTML tables in the current page's top-level document and reads their visible captions. It reads the visible text of the table you select and processes the preview, field selection and deduplication on your device. It does not read form values, sign in, crawl, or send table data to any server. Preview data is discarded when you close the popup. Export files remain in your downloads until you delete them. The extension stores only your preferred language in its browser storage. There is no account or analytics.

For privacy questions email dasean@yeah.net. If you contact us, we use the address and message you supply to respond.

隐私政策

点击查找时，扩展在当前页顶层文档中定位可见的 HTML 表格并读取可见标题。选择表格后，只读取其可见文本。预览、筛选及去重均在本机完成，扩展不读取表单值、不登录、不抓取其他页面、不上传。关闭弹窗后不保留预览，下载文件由你管理。扩展只在浏览器本地保存语言设置，无账户与分析追踪。隐私问题可发邮件至上述地址，来信内容及地址仅用于回复。
