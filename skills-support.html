KADA AI Skills Support

Open the library from the extension toolbar. Import a SKILL.md file, then select Save skill to keep it. To open the library in Chinese, choose 简体中文 from the language menu. Saved skills are specific to the browser profile where you saved them.

Email dasean@yeah.net with your browser name and version, extension version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Remove private text from any screenshot you send.

KADA AI Skills 技术支持

从扩展工具栏打开技能库，选择 SKILL.md 文件后点击「保存技能」。语言菜单可切换为简体中文。技能保存在当前浏览器配置中。
