KADA Workflow Privacy Policy

Kunlun Growth · Version 0.1.0

Recording only takes place on the extension's built-in practice board while you turn recording on. The recorder handles sample task labels, priority choices, and the Add and Complete buttons. It does not observe other browser tabs, websites or external fields. The board state and last replay log are held in the current page. Saving stores the edited workflow, including the sample labels, in this browser profile. The language preference is also local. Selecting Delete workflow removes the saved workflow. Uninstalling clears the extension's data from that profile.

There is no sign-in, analytics, or upload. If you email dasean@yeah.net, we use your email address and message to respond to your request.

隐私政策

只有你主动开启录制后，扩展才会在自带练习看板处理示例任务标签、优先级、添加及完成操作。扩展不观察其他标签页、网站或外部字段。看板及上次重放日志只保留在当前页面。手动保存会将包含示例标签的流程保存到当前浏览器配置，语言选择也保存在本地。点击「删除流程」可清除已保存的流程，卸载扩展可清除扩展数据。扩展没有登录、分析追踪或上传。来信的地址和内容用于回复。
